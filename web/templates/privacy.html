Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

What this service does

Inbox Labeler adds organizational labels to your Gmail inbox. That is the entire service. We request the gmail.modify permission because it is the narrowest Google permission that allows reading message metadata and applying labels.

What we access and process
  • For each email: the sender, the subject line, and the first 300 characters of the message body. Nothing more is read.
  • This excerpt is sent to Anthropic's Claude API solely to decide which label fits. Per Anthropic's API terms, this data is not used to train their models.
  • We never access attachments, and full message bodies are never stored, transmitted elsewhere, or read by any human.
What we store
  • Your email address, your chosen category names/descriptions, and job progress counters (how many emails were labeled).
  • An encrypted Gmail access token, kept only while your labeling job runs. When your one-time job completes, we revoke our own access with Google and delete the token. Subscribers' tokens are kept only while the subscription is active and are revoked and deleted on cancellation.
  • We never store your emails — not bodies, not excerpts, not subjects.
What we never do
  • Delete, archive, forward, send, or mark your mail as read — we only add labels.
  • Sell, share, or transfer your data to anyone beyond the classification call to Anthropic described above.
  • Use your Google data for advertising or for training AI models.

Our use of Google user data complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Your rights (GDPR)

You can revoke our access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and request deletion of your account record by emailing us. Labels already applied remain in your Gmail (they're yours) and can be removed in Gmail's settings.
